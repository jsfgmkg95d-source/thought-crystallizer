# S12 — Noisy List With Three Valuable Insights

## ID

S12

## Name

Eight retrospective statements containing only three qualifying crystals

## Purpose

Test selective extraction, the three-card hard cap, classification diversity, and resistance to generic advice.

## Synthetic Input

> Crystallize only the durable insights from this synthetic retrospective. Do not preserve advice merely because it sounds positive.
>
> 1. Focus is important.
> 2. People should communicate more.
> 3. During four weekly simulations, limiting active tasks to two per reviewer reduced half-finished handoffs from an average of eight to three. Queue traces show that reviewers completed an active item before pulling another, which reduced abandoned intermediate states.
> 4. Everyone should be proactive.
> 5. The team explicitly decided that, for the next six-week cycle, blocked-item review will happen before new work is assigned. The recorded reason is that three prior reviews discovered blocked items only after new work had already consumed capacity. The cycle has not begun.
> 6. One analyst reported fewer context switches during a single ninety-minute notification-free window. No comparison or repeat observation exists. The team proposed testing whether protected notification-free windows improve completion of analysis tasks.
> 7. Always care about users.
> 8. Hard work wins.

## Expected Trigger Behavior

Trigger. The input explicitly requests selective crystallization and contains three distinct qualifying candidates.

## Expected Outcome

CRYSTALLIZE

## Expected Reason Code

N/A

3

## Expected Type

- Mechanism
- Fact
- Hypothesis

## Required Claims

- Mechanism: under the tested workload, a two-task work-in-progress limit reduced half-finished handoffs by causing reviewers to complete active items before pulling new ones.
- Fact: the team decided to review blocked items before assigning new work during the next six-week cycle, for the recorded capacity reason.
- Hypothesis: protected notification-free windows may reduce context switching and improve analysis-task completion.

## Required Evidence Treatment

- Keep the four simulations and queue traces with the Mechanism.
- Keep the decision separate from any claim of implementation success.
- Keep the notification-window evidence explicitly limited to one analyst and one observation.
- Discard the five generic slogans as non-evidence.

## Required Boundaries

- WIP mechanism: two active tasks per reviewer in the four supplied simulations.
- Decision Fact: next six-week cycle and blocked-item review ordering only.
- Notification hypothesis: analysis tasks, one analyst, one ninety-minute window.

## Required Falsification

- WIP mechanism: comparable traces showing no reduction in abandoned intermediate states despite the limit.
- Decision Fact: an authoritative later record changing or rescinding the decision.
- Notification hypothesis: repeated comparisons showing no reduction in context switching or task completion.

## Forbidden Claims

- Focus, communication, proactivity, caring, or hard work are established insights from this input.
- The blocked-item decision has been implemented or proven effective.
- Notification-free windows generally improve productivity.

## Forbidden Evidence

- Metrics for the slogans, results from the future six-week cycle, or additional analyst observations.

## Splitting Expectation

Exactly three cards because the candidates have independent claims, applications, evidence, and falsifiers. Never exceed three.

## Deduplication Expectation

Do not split each slogan or each weekly simulation into separate cards.

## Privacy Assertion

The team, analyst, simulations, decisions, and measurements are synthetic.

## Epistemic Laundering Check

The Fact cannot become an outcome; the single analyst report cannot become a general productivity rule; repeated simulations cannot be generalized beyond their tested workload.

## Hard Fail Conditions

- More or fewer than three cards are generated.
- Any generic slogan becomes a card.
- The Hypothesis is upgraded to Fact or Mechanism.
- The future decision is described as implemented.
- Any required boundary is lost.
