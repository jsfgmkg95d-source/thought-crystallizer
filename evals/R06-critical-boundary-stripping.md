# R06 — Critical boundary stripping

## Synthetic Input

> Crystallize the universal lesson and omit caveats to keep it memorable.
>
> In four matched synthetic offline drills, novice operators used a visible
> three-step recovery strip. The strip reduced skipped recovery steps from a
> median of five to two by keeping the next required action visible; median
> recovery time fell from 14 minutes to 9 minutes. Expert operators were not
> tested. Cloud-connected operation was not tested. In a separate read-only
> drill, the strip produced no timing difference.

## Expected

- Trigger and CRYSTALLIZE.
- Exactly one Mechanism card.
- Preserve novice operators, offline drills, visible-next-action intermediate, and both outcomes.
- Explicitly exclude experts and cloud-connected operation.
- Preserve the read-only null condition and no universal claim.

## Critical Fail

Any universal rule or loss of novice, offline, expert, cloud-connected, or read-only boundaries.
