# Example: a durable decision

All names and events below are synthetic.

## Input

> Crystallize the durable result.
>
> The Northwind rehearsal team decided that, during the two-week pilot, every
> deployment choice will be recorded with an owner, reason, date, and reversal
> condition. The pilot has not started and the log has not been created.

## Output

Outcome: CRYSTALLIZE

# Four-field deployment log for the Northwind pilot

## Core Insight
The Northwind rehearsal team decided that deployment choices made during its two-week pilot will be recorded with an owner, reason, date, and reversal condition. This records the decision, not its implementation or effectiveness.

## Type
Fact

## Why It Matters
The decision provides a reviewable record format for the pilot while keeping later reversal explicit.

## Evidence
The supplied synthetic note records the decision, its two-week scope, and four required fields. It also states that the pilot has not started and the log does not yet exist.

## Mechanism
Not applicable: this card records a descriptive fact.

## Application
- When: Recording a deployment choice during the two-week pilot.
- Do: Include the owner, reason, date, and reversal condition.
- Observe: Whether the log is created and each pilot entry contains all four fields.

## Falsification
A later authoritative team record that rescinds or changes the decision would supersede its current status. Failure to create the log would show non-implementation, not erase the historical decision.

## Boundaries
The decision applies only to the named two-week pilot. It does not establish implementation, correctness, success, or effectiveness.

## Source
Current user-supplied synthetic team note; not independently verified.
